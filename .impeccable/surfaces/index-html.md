# Surface: index.html (Штаб FLL, whole app)

Mode: Operate. Audience: FLL school teams in Kazakhstan (kids 9–16, captain, mentor), at a desk on a computer in daylight. Job: plan tasks, log training runs, collect Core Values stories, manage the team. Must keep every element id and behaviour.

## Direction contract

THESIS: the category standard played straight at Notion's craft level: a calm light workspace where the team's board, runs and stories read instantly. Refuses dark glow and novelty costumes; the one decoration is the navy header with sticker dots, chosen by the user.

OWN-WORLD: white canvas, warm off-white side surfaces (#f7f6f3), warm hairlines (#e9e9e7), warm charcoal text (#37352f), Notion's pastel tag palette (gray, blue, yellow, green, red, purple, orange, pink) for statuses, categories, roles and score zones; one purple primary (#5645d4) for main actions. Inter throughout with tabular numerals; soft 1px ring + small shadow on cards; 6–8px radii.

STORY: a member sees the four status columns as Notion board groups, takes a task, logs a run on the training page and watches the score line climb toward the goal.

FIRST VIEWPORT: full-bleed deep navy header band (#0a1530, Notion's brand navy) with the workspace icon, «Штаб FLL», team name and user on the left, Notion brand-colour sticker dots in the free space, and the countdown in a translucent card on the right; below it the light workspace: view tabs underlined; page title; add-task row; board with status pills (Свободные gray, В работе blue, На проверке yellow, Принято green) and white cards with pastel category tags.

AMENDMENT (user, after first review): asked «сделай более интересный дизайн»; from the offered options chose only «Цветная шапка» (navy band with sticker dots). Declined: coloured columns, multi-coloured Core Values tiles, tab icons.

FORM: seed: none (pinned) — user: «не надо в стиле клетки, сделай в каком-то норм стиле»; chose «Notion» as the reference.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
